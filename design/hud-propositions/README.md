# Propositions de HUD

Trois planches conceptuelles générées avec l’outil intégré ImageGen, à partir de la référence fournie et du décor pixel art actuel. Elles ne modifient pas le HUD du jeu. Les ressources, la construction, les objectifs et la mini-carte représentés sont des propositions de design. Les valeurs restent illustratives.

## Prompts de génération

Chaque prompt est constitué du tronc commun ci-dessous suivi de la variante correspondante.

### Tronc commun

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE polished landscape 1920x1280 game HUD art-direction presentation board in French for EXILÉS / SANCTUM LACERTAE. Input image 1 is the ART DIRECTION REFERENCE ONLY: charcoal translucent panels, aged brass thin edges, ivory serif typography, amber runes, restrained sacred medieval military aesthetic. Input image 2 is the ACTUAL CURRENT PIXEL ART GAME: keep its jungle, isometric temple layout, portal, purple anchor and single engineer as the recognizable gameplay backdrop, crisp pixel art. Do not turn terrain into realistic 3D. We are designing HUD proposals, not new game environments. Board composition: small elegant title and one-line design description at top; one large 16:9 gameplay HUD mockup taking about 75% of board width and height; bottom strip with 3 enlarged component studies titled 'SÉLECTION', 'COMMANDES', 'ALERTES'. Keep coherent consistent UI between gameplay view and component strip. Strong legibility and spacing, restrained ornament, professional production game interface concept sheet, no device mockup or watermark. HUD needs top resource counters Fer 1240 / Bois 820 / Esprit 310 / Population 1/40; wave 2/5 and next assault 04:17; anchor integrity100%; objectives compact upper right; mini map bottom left; single selected Ingénieur Exilé with 140/140 HP; actions Construire B, Réparer R, Position H, Déplacer; tooltip Construire with Fer80 Bois40 Temps30s; keyboard badges; show hover amber, disabled desaturated, active gold states. Resource and construction features are design proposals. No Indra soldiers on the terrain or roster; engineer only. Use French labels with accurate accents, avoid paragraphs and tiny illegible text.

### 01-heritage-tyrien.png

Title '01 — HÉRITAGE TYRIEN'. Closest to reference. Traditional continuous bottom RTS dashboard spanning screen with minimap left, rich helmet portrait and identity center-left, stats center, 2x4 square command grid right. Top wide black/brass resource ribbon, upper left wave inset. Thin carved brass corners, subtle black stone textures, serif uppercase smallcaps. Bottom HUD occupies at most 23% of gameplay screen. Amber sacred rune as selected unit emblem. Strong visual craft, balanced spacious panels.

### 02-sanctum-epure.png

Title '02 — SANCTUM ÉPURÉ'. Minimal refined floating modular HUD. Very thin brass outlines, flat smoked dark green glass panels, minimal texture, fine ivory type. Top compact centered resource capsule. Upper left two tiny wave and anchor cards; upper right objectives. Minimap lower left as small square; selected engineer small portrait + narrow HP card lower center; 4 large readable actions in floating horizontal toolbar lower right. At least 80% of world stays unobstructed. Large calm negative space, clean practical UI. Retain fantasy identity with rune geometry and precise brass detailing, no sci-fi holograms.

### 03-commandement.png

Title '03 — COMMANDEMENT'. Tactical compact military fantasy HUD. Asymmetrical UI: left vertical sidebar has top wave countdown and anchor integrity, small objectives below, minimap at bottom; top thin resource line; lower right a strong L-shaped selected engineer card plus command grid. Very clear hierarchy with wave threat orange, healing green, selection brass. Squared cut corners, etched dark steel and bronze, ivory utilitarian labels paired with serif titles, minimal medieval ornament. Bottom center remains open for gameplay. Show conspicuous compact 'PROCHAIN ASSAUT · 04:17' alert and keyboard access. Professional readable RTS command console.
